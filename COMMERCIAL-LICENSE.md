## Which one applies to you

The licence permits any **noncommercial purpose**. It names two cases that are
always permitted:

- **personal use** — research, experiment, study, private entertainment and
  hobby projects, with no anticipated commercial application;
- **use by a noncommercial organisation** — a charity, an educational
  institution, a public research organisation, a public safety, health or
  environmental organisation, or a government institution.

Anything commercial is outside it. In practice you need a commercial licence
if you, or the company you work for:

- use this in the course of a business — to build products, serve clients, or
  run the company's own work, including internally;
- offer it, or something built on it, as part of a commercial product or
  service;
- sell, bundle or redistribute it, or a modified version, commercially.

If you are not sure which side of the line you are on, ask before you rely on
it. The text in [LICENSE](LICENSE) is what governs; this page is a
plain-language summary and does not change it.

## Getting a commercial licence

Open an issue titled **Commercial licence** at
<https://github.com/s-rakim/collaboration-des-esprits/issues>, or contact the
owner through <https://github.com/s-rakim>. Say who would be using it, roughly
how (internal tool, part of a product, hosted for others), and how many people.

A commercial licence is a separate written agreement. Until you have one,
signed, commercial use is not permitted.
